You receive each stipend installment by submitting an expense report in Expensify, the expense tool the Linux Foundation uses to pay stipends. These steps apply to mentees accepted onto a funded program; the last section applies to Program Admins.

## Before you begin

- You have received a satisfactory midterm or final evaluation. Submit one report per installment, after each evaluation.
- You have an Expensify account. Sign in with the same email address you used to set it up.
- Know your total stipend from [Stipend Amounts by Country](../stipend-amounts/). Each report is for half of it.
- **Outside the United States:** fill in the [Wire Transfer Form](/files/docs/stipends/lf-wire-transfer-form.pdf). The Linux Foundation pays you using those bank details.
- **In the United States:** add your US bank deposit account in Expensify, under **Settings** → **Account** → **Payments** → **Bank Accounts**.

## Steps

1. Sign in to [Expensify](https://www.expensify.com).
2. Select **+** → **Create expense**.
3. Fill in the expense:
   - **Amount:** half of your total stipend.
   - **Merchant:** your name. If you live outside the United States, add "International Bank" after your name.
   - **Date:** today's date.
   - **Category:** **Mentorship**.
   - **Projects:** the mentorship program you were accepted onto. A wrong program makes the report invalid.
   - **Description:** the program name and the installment, for example "Linux Kernel - mentee stipend payment 1 of 2".
4. If you live outside the United States, attach the completed Wire Transfer Form as the receipt. If Expensify also asks for your bank details, add them, since that is usually the fastest way to be paid.
5. Select **Create expense**, then **Submit**.

## After completing

Your Program Admin reviews and approves the report, then a Linux Foundation administrator validates it, and Expensify pays you. You are notified when it is approved.

Payments can take time to arrive:

- An international wire transfer can take up to six weeks from approval, and sometimes more than 30 business days to be released by a bank outside the United States.
- Even when Expensify shows the report as **Reimbursed**, the money can take another three to four weeks to reach your account.
- If Expensify shows an expected payment date, wait at least one week past it before asking about the payment.
- Missing or wrong bank details delay the payment further.

## For Program Admins: approve a stipend report

When a mentee submits a report, you receive an email from Expensify.

1. Open the email and select **Approve**. Expensify opens the report.
2. Check the amount against the mentee's country in [Stipend Amounts by Country](../stipend-amounts/): each installment is half the total.
3. Approve the report, or decline it if the amount is wrong (for example, the full stipend requested in the first installment) or the mentee has not completed their assigned tasks.
