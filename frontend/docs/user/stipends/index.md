Mentees accepted onto a funded LFX Mentorship program receive a stipend, paid in two installments after the midterm and final evaluations. The amount depends on the country you live in. This page applies to accepted mentees and to the Program Admins who approve their payments.

## How much is the stipend?

See [Stipend Amounts by Country](./stipend-amounts/) for the total for each country. Your country is the one you currently live in. If you study at an online institution or recently graduated, the stipend is based on your permanent residence.

## How is the amount calculated?

The Linux Foundation uses a Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) calculation. PPP compares what the same goods cost in different countries. The calculation starts from a base of 6,000 USD and adjusts it by each country's PPP value, with a minimum of 1,000 USD and a maximum of 6,600 USD. The base allows for the fact that many universities are in cities, which cost more than the rest of a country. The amounts are reviewed regularly.

## When is the stipend paid?

The stipend is paid in two equal installments:

| Installment | When                                                 | Amount         |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| **1 of 2**  | After a satisfactory midterm evaluation              | Half the total |
| **2 of 2**  | After a satisfactory final evaluation, at graduation | Half the total |

You must receive a satisfactory evaluation before you request each installment.

## How do I get paid?

You submit an expense report for each installment. See [Get Paid](./get-paid/).
