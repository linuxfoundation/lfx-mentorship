Accepted mentees in most programs receive a stipend. The amount depends on the country where you live, and it is paid in two halves after your midterm and final evaluations.

## How much will I receive?

See [Stipend amounts by country](./amounts/). Your country is where you currently live. If you study at an online institution or recently graduated, your stipend is based on your permanent residence.

## How are the amounts calculated?

The Linux Foundation uses a Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) calculation. It starts from a base of 6,000 USD and adjusts it by each country's PPP value, with a minimum of 1,000 USD and a maximum of 6,600 USD. The base allows for the fact that many universities are in cities, which cost more than other parts of a country. The amounts are reviewed regularly.

Purchasing Power Parity estimates the exchange rate at which two currencies buy the same amount of goods. See [Purchasing power parity on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity).

## When is the stipend paid?

Each payment depends on satisfactory progress:

- **Full-time terms (12 weeks):** half after the midterm evaluation at the end of week 6, and half after the final evaluation at the end of week 12.
- **Part-time terms (24 weeks):** half after the midterm evaluation at week 12, and half after the final evaluation at week 24.

The Linux Foundation's HR and finance teams handle contracts and payments, and they contact accepted mentees directly.
