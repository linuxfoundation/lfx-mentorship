The mentor directory is the public list of everyone mentoring on LFX Mentorship.

## Before you begin

No sign-in is required.

## Steps

1. Select **Mentors** from the top navigation.

   ![Mentor directory](/images/docs/screenshots/mentors/mentors-directory.png)

2. Use **All skills** to show only mentors who list a given skill.
3. Each card shows the mentor's name, how long they have been mentoring, their introduction and their skills.
4. Select **View Profile** to open the full profile.

   ![Mentor profile](/images/docs/screenshots/mentors/mentor-profile.png)

5. The profile shows their introduction, skills, links, and the programs they mentor.
