Mentors volunteer their time to guide mentees through a program. You work in the [Mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor) area of LFX Self Serve.

## Create your mentor profile

Open the [Mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor) area. The first time, you fill in:

- **Introduction**: tell us a little about yourself.
- **Skills**: which of your skills to feature on your public mentor page.
- **Compliance Confirmation**, and agreement to the Terms and Conditions.

Select **Submit**. Your public mentor page then shows on the Mentorship site.

## Join a program

There are two ways to mentor a program:

- **Request to join.** In your mentor profile, under **Mentorship Program Details**, select the program. The program admins approve or decline your request. Until then you can select **Withdraw Request**.
- **Accept an invitation.** A program admin invites you, and you get an email. Open the link, sign in, and select **Accept** or **Decline**. If the page says the invitation is for a different account, sign in with the LFX account the email was sent to. If the link has expired or was already answered, ask the program admin to invite you again.

## Your programs

**Programs** lists the programs you mentor, with counts of mentees, applicants and tasks to review. Open a program to see three tabs:

- **Applicants**: the mentees who applied, with their prerequisite tasks and their other active applications. You can add notes to help the program admins decide.
- **Mentees**: the accepted mentees and their progress.
- **Tasks**: the tasks your mentees submitted. Filter by review status and open a task to review it.

Program admins make the final decision to accept or decline an application.

## Update your mentor profile

Open **Profile** and select **Edit Mentor Profile**. Your name and photo come from your LFX profile; select **Edit LFX Profile** to change them.
