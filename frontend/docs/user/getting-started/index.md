LFX Mentorship pairs people who want to learn open source with experienced mentors on Linux Foundation projects. Program admins run programs, mentors guide the work, and mentees contribute to a project for a term, usually with a stipend.

## Where things happen

LFX Mentorship has two parts:

- **The Mentorship site** is public. Anyone can find programs, read program details, and view mentee and mentor profiles. You don't sign in there.
- **LFX Self Serve** at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) is where you sign in with your LFX account to apply, mentor, or run a program. Its Mentorship section has three areas: [Mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee), [Mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor) and [Admin](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/admin).

## Who takes part

- **Mentees** apply to programs, complete prerequisite tasks, and work with a mentor during the term. See [Mentees](../mentees/).
- **Mentors** volunteer their time to guide mentees. They review applications and tasks for the programs they mentor. See [Mentors](../mentors/).
- **Program admins** enroll a program, set its terms and prerequisites, invite mentors, and accept mentees. They are the single point of contact for mentors, mentees, and the Linux Foundation's HR and finance teams. See [Program admins](../program-admins/).

## How a program runs

1. A program admin enrolls a program. The Linux Foundation reviews it, and once it is approved it appears on the Mentorship site.
2. The program opens a term for applications.
3. Mentees apply and complete the prerequisite tasks. Mentors and program admins review the applications.
4. Program admins accept mentees for the term. The Linux Foundation's HR team then contacts accepted mentees to complete onboarding.
5. During the term, mentors evaluate each mentee's progress. Stipend payments follow the midterm and final evaluations.
6. Mentees who complete the program graduate and receive a Letter of Completion.

## Terms and timelines

Programs can name their terms and set their own dates. Most full-time programs follow this schedule:

| Term   | Dates                     | Programs listed | Applications open |
| ------ | ------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| Spring | March 1 – May 31          | mid-January     | about 4 weeks     |
| Summer | June 1 – August 31        | mid-April       | about 4 weeks     |
| Fall   | September 1 – November 30 | mid-July        | about 4 weeks     |

Program admins review and accept mentees about 2 weeks before the term begins. Part-time terms run for three to six months, depending on the program.

A full-time term lasts 12 weeks. The midterm evaluation is due at the end of week 6 and the final evaluation at the end of week 12; each one approves half of the stipend. A part-time term lasts 24 weeks, with the midterm at week 12 and the final at week 24. Mentors and program admins may also run optional evaluations in between.

## Getting help

Select **Contact support** at the bottom of the Mentorship site. It takes you to LFX Self Serve, where you can chat with support after signing in.
