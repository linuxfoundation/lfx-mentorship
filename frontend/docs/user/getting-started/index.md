LFX Mentorship pairs contributors with experienced members of open source projects. Over a multi-week term you take on real, scoped work from the project's backlog and review it with a named mentor.

![LFX Mentorship home page](/images/docs/screenshots/getting-started/home-hero.png)

## Who takes part

| Role | What they do | Where they work |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentee** | Applies to a program, completes scoped tasks across the term, and takes a final evaluation | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee) |
| **Mentor** | Scopes the work, reviews what a mentee submits, and scores applicants | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor) |
| **Program Admin** | Enrolls a project's program, invites mentors, and runs the term | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/admin](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/admin) |

Browsing programs, mentees and mentors on this site needs no account. Applying, mentoring or administering a program all happen in LFX and need an [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/).

## How a term works

![How It Works](/images/docs/screenshots/getting-started/home-how-it-works.png)

1. **Build your profile** — add your skills, links and a short introduction. Mentors read this first.
2. **Apply to programs** — up to three applications at a time. Each is reviewed by the program's mentors.
3. **Get matched** — mentors score applicants on skills fit and intent, then accept for the term.
4. **Do the work** — scoped tasks with regular reviews from your mentor.
5. **Graduate** — pass the final evaluation and receive your stipend installments, where the program is funded.

## Who can apply

![Who Can Apply](/images/docs/screenshots/getting-started/home-who-can-apply.png)

- You are 18 or older.
- You can commit consistent hours for the full term.
- You may have up to **3 applications** open at a time.
- You may only complete **one** LFX Mentorship program in your career.

Not every program is funded. Where a stipend is offered, the amount varies by program and by your country of residence, and the program page states it before you apply.

## Key terms

- **Program** — a project's mentorship offering. A program belongs to one Linux Foundation project and lists the skills it is looking for.
- **Term** — the dated run of a program. A program can run several terms over the years; each has its own dates and its own mentees.
- **Application** — a mentee's or mentor's request to join a program for a term. An application that is turned down is **declined**.
- **Program status** — where a program sits in moderation. A program that is not approved is **rejected**; that word is never used for an application.
- **Prerequisite** — something a candidate must supply before an application counts as complete, such as a resume, a cover letter or a coding challenge.
- **Stipend** — the payment some programs make to a mentee in milestone installments.
