Answers to common questions about using LFX Mentorship.

## Do I need a new account?

No. Sign in to [LFX Self Serve](https://app.lfx.dev) with your existing LFX account, the same one you used on the old LFX Mentorship site.

## Where did the old LFX Mentorship site go?

The old site at mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org was replaced by the new Mentorship site and LFX Self Serve. Old addresses redirect automatically. Public pages, such as a program, mentee or mentor page, open the same page on the new site. Pages that needed a sign-in, such as your applications or tasks, open the matching page in LFX Self Serve.

## Was my data moved to the new site?

Yes. Programs, terms, mentee and mentor profiles, applications and tasks were moved. Two things were not:

- **Resumes** uploaded to the old site.
- **Pending mentor invitations.** If you were invited to mentor a program but had not answered yet, ask the program admin to invite you again.

## A link in an old email doesn't work. What do I do?

Links to programs, mentees and mentors keep working. Links that asked you to sign in take you to the matching page in LFX Self Serve. Invitation links from the old site no longer work; ask the program admin to send a new invitation.

## How do I see or change my profile?

Sign in to LFX Self Serve and open [Mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee) or [Mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor), then open **Profile**. Your name and photo come from your LFX profile; select **Edit LFX Profile** to change them.

## How do I contact support?

Select **Contact support** at the bottom of the Mentorship site. It takes you to LFX Self Serve, where you can chat with support after signing in.
