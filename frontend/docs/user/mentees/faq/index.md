## How many programs can I apply to at once?

You can have up to three applications open at a time. Withdrawing an application frees a slot straight away.

## Can I apply to the same program twice?

You can apply to a program again in a later term. You cannot have two open applications to the same term.

## How do I withdraw an application?

In LFX, the Linux Foundation's software platform, open your mentee overview at [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee) and withdraw from the application. Withdrawing frees one of your three slots.

## My application says "declined" — can I reapply?

Yes, you can reapply — to a different program, or to a later term of the same program. **Declined** is the status for any application that is not taken on; it is not a ban.

## Can I find out why I was not selected?

No. Programs receive many more applications than they can accept, so mentors and program staff cannot give individual feedback, and selection decisions are final. See the [Standards of Excellence](../../getting-started/standards-of-excellence/).

## I withdrew, or was declined, by mistake — can it be undone?

Yes. Ask a Program Admin of the program to re-open the application. It goes back to **Pending**, and they decide on it again. You cannot re-open it yourself, and no email is sent. Your tasks stay as they were, and if the program added a prerequisite in the meantime, you get a task for it.

A re-opened application counts as one of your three open applications again, so it can't be re-opened while you already have three. It also can't be re-opened once the term has closed, or while you have another open application to the same term.

## I was marked as graduated by mistake — can it be undone?

Yes. A Program Admin of the program can undo the graduation, which puts you back to **Accepted** on the term. No email is sent. It can't be undone if you have since been accepted onto another program, or once the term has ended.

## Where are my tasks?

Under **My Tasks** in LFX at [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee). Tasks set before you are accepted are part of your application; tasks set after you are accepted are the term's work.

## How am I evaluated?

Your mentor evaluates your progress during the term, with a midterm and a final evaluation. In a 12-week full-time term these usually fall at the end of weeks 6 and 12, with optional check-ins at the end of weeks 3 and 9. A 24-week part-time term doubles each: weeks 12 and 24, with optional check-ins at weeks 6 and 18. How each evaluation is done — a 1:1 check-in, a meeting or a written evaluation — is up to the program.

Your mentor's evaluations are final. You can ask clarifying questions, but not dispute them; see the [Standards of Excellence](../../getting-started/standards-of-excellence/).

## Can I do a second mentorship?

You can take part in only one LFX Mentorship program in your career.

## When do I get paid?

Where a program is funded, the stipend is paid in two installments, after a satisfactory midterm evaluation and after the final evaluation. You request each one with an expense report; see [Get Paid](../../stipends/get-paid/). The amount depends on your country of residence; see [Stipend Amounts by Country](../../stipends/stipend-amounts/).

## Why is my profile not in the mentee directory?

The directory lists mentees who have joined a program. Creating a profile alone does not list you.
