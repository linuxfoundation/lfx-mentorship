You find programs on the Mentorship site and apply in LFX Self Serve with your LFX account.

## 1. Find a program

On the Mentorship site, open **Find a Program**. Search or filter the list, then open a program to read its description, required skills, terms and prerequisites. Check that the term you want is accepting applications.

## 2. Start your application

On the program page, select **Apply to This Program**. In the **Sign in to apply** window, select **Sign In**, or **Create an Account** if you don't have an LFX account yet. LFX Self Serve opens the application for that program in a new tab.

## 3. Create your mentee profile

The first time you apply, you create your mentee profile:

- **Introduction**: tell us a little about yourself.
- **Skills**: choose the skills you bring.
- Your country and the demographic questions.
- **Compliance Confirmation**, and agreement to the Terms and Conditions: the LFX Platform Use Agreement, the Service-Specific Use Terms, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Privacy Policy.

Select **Submit** to save the profile. You only do this once; later applications reuse it.

## 4. Submit the application

Choose the term, review the prerequisites, and select **Submit Application**.

If you see **This term is not accepting applications**, the term's application window is closed. Check the program page for other terms.

## 5. Complete the prerequisite tasks

Programs list prerequisite tasks that you complete while your application is reviewed. Open **Tasks** in the [Mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee) area, open each task, and upload your work. Applications without completed prerequisites are not accepted.

Reviews take at least two weeks. See [Track your applications and tasks](../applications-and-tasks/) for what happens next.
