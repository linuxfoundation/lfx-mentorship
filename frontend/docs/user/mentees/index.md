As a mentee, you contribute to an open source project for a term while an experienced mentor guides you. You apply to programs in LFX Self Serve, complete the program's prerequisite tasks, and, once accepted, work through the term's tasks and evaluations.

## Who can apply

All mentee applicants must:

- Be at least 18 years old by the time the program starts.
- Not be a prior or active participant in another Linux Foundation mentorship program.
- Be eligible to work in the country and jurisdiction where you will take part in the program.
- Not live in a country or jurisdiction where taking part is prohibited under applicable U.S. federal, state or local laws, or the laws of other countries.
- Apply on your own behalf, as an individual.
- Not be subject to obligations to third parties, such as an employment contract, that would restrict or prohibit your participation.
- Meet every requirement set by the program, including its custom prerequisites.
