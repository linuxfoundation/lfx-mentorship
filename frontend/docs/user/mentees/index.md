A **mentee** is a contributor taking part in a program for one term. Mentees do scoped work from the project's backlog and review it with a named mentor.

## What you can do

- Create a mentee profile and apply to up to three programs at a time
- Track your applications and complete the tasks a program sets
- Browse the public mentee directory and read mentee profiles

## Eligibility

- You are 18 or older.
- You can commit consistent hours for the full term.
- You may have up to **3** open applications at a time.
- You may only complete **one** LFX Mentorship program in your career.

## Where things happen

Browsing the mentee directory happens here. Everything else — your profile, your applications and your tasks — happens in LFX, the Linux Foundation's software platform, at [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee).
