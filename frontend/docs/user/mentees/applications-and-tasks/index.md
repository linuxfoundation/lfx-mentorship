Everything you do as a mentee after applying happens in the [Mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee) area of LFX Self Serve. It has three tabs: **Overview**, **Tasks** and **Profile**.

## Check an application's status

**Overview** lists your applications with the program, the date you submitted, and the outcome. An application can be:

| Status    | Meaning                                                                                       |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending   | Submitted and waiting for review.                                                             |
| Accepted  | You were accepted for the term. The Linux Foundation's HR team contacts you about onboarding. |
| Declined  | The program did not accept your application.                                                  |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew the application.                                                                 |
| Graduated | You completed the program.                                                                    |

You also get an email when your application is accepted or declined.

## Submit tasks

**Tasks** shows your prerequisite tasks and, once you are accepted, the tasks your mentor assigns during the term, with your progress. Open a task to read what is needed, upload your work, and submit it. Your mentor reviews each submission.

## Withdraw an application

Open **Profile**, find the application in your application history, and select **Withdraw Application**.

## Update your mentee profile

Open **Profile** and select **Edit Mentee Profile** to change your introduction and skills. Your name and photo come from your LFX profile; select **Edit LFX Profile** to change them.
