Your mentee profile is what program mentors read first. It has to exist before you can apply to anything.

## Before you begin

- You need an [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/). Creating one is free.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Have your skills, links and a short introduction ready.

## Steps

1. Select **My Mentorship** in the top navigation, or go straight to [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee).
2. Sign in with your LF ID.
3. Fill in the mentee registration form. It asks for your introduction, your skills, and the links a mentor would want to see.
4. Save the profile.

## After completing

Your profile is live and you can apply to programs. If you already had a profile, opening the same link takes you straight to your mentee overview instead of the form.
