A Program Admin can invite you to mentor their program by email. The invitation is answered in LFX, not in the email itself.

## Before you begin

- The invitation email contains a one-time link. Use the link rather than navigating by hand.
- You need an [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/). If you do not have a mentor profile yet, you are asked to create one.

## Steps

1. Open the invitation email and select its link. It takes you to the invitation page in LFX.
2. Sign in with your LF ID if you are not already signed in.
3. Review the program named on the page.
4. Select **Accept** or **Decline**.

Nothing is answered until you make that choice on the page, so a mail scanner that follows the link cannot accept or decline on your behalf.

## After completing

Accepting puts the program under **My Programs** in LFX, where its mentees and tasks appear. Declining leaves the program unchanged and notifies the Program Admin.
